# P3 Health Lab — Animated Project Film

## Director's treatment

- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, designed for a 1920×1080 capture.
- **Promise:** P3 Health Lab connects the air around us to the health of people and places, then turns evidence into practical action.
- **Audience:** Research partners, students, funders, public-health practitioners, and people discovering the lab for the first time.
- **Identity:** A quiet academic palette derived from the P3 site: warm paper cream, deep ink blue, muted teal, leaf green, and a restrained coral accent. Use Space Grotesk for display type, DM Sans for supporting copy, and JetBrains Mono for labels.
- **Hero:** The supplied city-and-community photograph is treated as a living environmental-health landscape, not as a static website banner. The supplied P3 logo is reserved for the conclusion.
- **Motion:** Cropped photographic parallax, circular/radial air-flow geometry, and evidence-like line systems. Cuts use a shared ring, masked split, or scale-through so each transition has a physical carrier.
- **Arc:** Begin with a human-scale question, widen to the air and built environment, turn observation into evidence, return to shared community, and resolve into the lab's mission.
- **Payoff:** The ending is not another information card: the field darkens, the logo becomes the only bright anchor, and the full mission line holds long enough to be remembered.
- **Audio:** One instrumental, cinematic-minimal bed. No voiceover, sound effects, or sung vocals. Musical alignment is unverified until the generated bed exists.

## Complete shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.20s / 4.20s — “THE AIR AROUND US”

- **Purpose:** Establish that environmental health begins with an everyday shared condition, not an abstract institution.
- **Exact content:** At 0.30s, `THE AIR AROUND US`; at 1.05s, `SHAPES HOW WE LIVE.`; at 2.10s, the mono label `P3 HEALTH LAB / KIGALI`; all copy holds through 3.55s, then travels out with the handoff.
- **Opening composition:** Warm cream field fills the frame. A narrow slice of the supplied city photograph is already visible from x=58% to the right edge, cropped to show the skyline and people without exposing the full image. A thin teal baseline runs from x=53% to x=92% at y=72%.
- **Ending composition:** The photograph has expanded to roughly 70% of the frame width. Three translucent teal rings sit over the skyline, centered at x=75%, y=49%. The second ring is the transition carrier.
- **Artistic idea:** The image enters as a horizon, then behaves like a shared atmosphere: the crop rises a few percent while rings expand from the same point, making a social landscape feel measurable without turning it into a chart.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35s: the photo is on-screen, sharp, with a subtle scale from 1.04 to 1.00; the baseline draws left-to-right. 0.25–0.95s: `THE AIR AROUND US` reveals through a vertical mask from the cream zone. 0.75–1.45s: `SHAPES HOW WE LIVE.` resolves below it with a 20px upward settle. 1.45–2.25s: the mono label appears as a small tracking expansion. 1.85–3.25s: three rings pulse outward one at a time; the image parallax shifts left 2%. 3.25–4.20s: the largest ring scales toward 1.45x and crosses the frame centre while type compresses and fades, setting up the next scene.
- **Transition contract:** The largest ring remains the same teal ring in Shot 02. The outgoing ring centre and incoming AIR word share the right-side focal point. Use simultaneous overlap, not a blank dissolve.
- **Assets and technique:** Existing city/community photo imported from `attached_assets/IMG-20260925-WA0006_1790363975752.jpg`; CSS mask reveal; SVG circles and baseline; no generated assets.
- **Sound intent:** Music begins with a restrained pulse; the first ring expansion should meet the first clear musical lift if available, but alignment remains unverified.

### Shot 02 / 4.20–9.40s / 5.20s — “AIR / PLACE”

- **Purpose:** Move from the broad question to the relationship between air, buildings, streets, and people.
- **Exact content:** At 4.45s, `AIR`; at 5.25s, `PLACE`; at 6.55s, `WHERE HEALTH BEGINS.`; at 7.20s, the labels `BUILT ENVIRONMENT` and `EVERYDAY EXPOSURE`; all hold until the outgoing scale-through.
- **Opening composition:** The photo is full-bleed, with the building and sky weighted to the upper-right and people retained in the lower-right. The ring from Shot 01 is a large, partially cropped outline around the word AIR.
- **Ending composition:** The photo has pushed into a close crop of the skyline/people boundary; AIR and PLACE sit on separate depth planes, with AIR behind the ring and PLACE crossing its lower edge. A thin moving contour line runs diagonally toward the lower-left label zone.
- **Artistic idea:** The word AIR is not a title beside a picture; it is a transparent measuring lens over the picture. PLACE is physically anchored to the lower edge of that lens, turning the crop into a relationship.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.50s: the inherited ring completes its scale-through and settles at 1.00x; AIR is visible within 0.15s, entering with a slight blur-to-sharp. 0.50–1.20s: AIR expands to 24vmin and the photo makes a measured 3% push-in. 1.10–1.85s: PLACE rises from below the ring, offset 8px behind AIR. 1.85–2.55s: a contour line draws from the ring toward the lower-left, bending once around the photo's building edge. 2.35–3.10s: `WHERE HEALTH BEGINS.` appears in the lower-left safe zone. 3.10–4.30s: the two mono labels arrive one at a time on opposite sides of the line; the second is delayed 180ms. 4.30–5.20s: the contour line straightens, becomes a bright horizontal trace, and wipes the photo upward as the words split vertically.
- **Transition contract:** The horizontal trace becomes the first line of Shot 03's evidence system. The ring exits as a thin outline at the centre, while the next scene's dark field is already visible under the upward photo wipe.
- **Assets and technique:** Same supplied photo, second and final appearance; CSS object-position and scale; SVG contour path with stroke-dashoffset; masked split transition; no new photo texture.
- **Sound intent:** Music adds a second layer during the contour draw; no spoken words.

### Shot 03 / 9.40–15.00s / 5.60s — “EVIDENCE MOVES”

- **Purpose:** Show the lab's work as active research that connects observation, teaching, and action.
- **Exact content:** At 9.55s, `EVIDENCE MOVES`; at 10.35s, the mono label `RESEARCH / TEACHING / ACTION`; at 11.20s, `SEE THE PATTERN.`; at 12.65s, `FOLLOW THE SIGNAL.`; all copy remains legible through 14.55s.
- **Opening composition:** Deep ink-blue field. The inherited trace enters from the left at y=46%, with a bright node at x=31%. Two small empty nodes are already visible at x=57% and x=76%, avoiding an empty incoming frame.
- **Ending composition:** The trace has become a three-branch network with nodes at distinct vertical levels. `RESEARCH`, `TEACHING`, and `ACTION` occupy three non-overlapping zones: upper-left, lower-left, and right-middle. A small coral accent travels along the final branch.
- **Artistic idea:** The line is an evidence path, not decoration. It grows from one observation into a connected system, and each word appears only when the trace reaches its node.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.40s: the inherited horizontal trace snaps into the first segment; the first node brightens. 0.40–1.15s: a new branch draws up-right, followed 180ms later by a lower branch. 0.75–1.35s: `EVIDENCE MOVES` enters as two lines on a clipped vertical reveal, anchored to the upper-left branch. 1.35–2.20s: the three labels appear sequentially as the branches reach their nodes; no stacked simultaneous callouts. 2.20–3.05s: `SEE THE PATTERN.` replaces the headline through a scale/blur word exchange while the network continues drawing. 3.05–4.05s: a coral signal dot travels the upper branch, pauses at the centre node, then continues to the right. 4.05–5.00s: `FOLLOW THE SIGNAL.` resolves near the right node; branch opacity rises to a readable settled state. 5.00–5.60s: all three branches arc inward and form a teal circle, with the coral dot becoming its leading edge.
- **Transition contract:** The circle formed by the network is the opening aperture for Shot 04. The dark field remains behind it for the first few frames, then the cream field unfolds from inside the circle.
- **Assets and technique:** Pure CSS/SVG evidence trace, deterministic node positions, stroke-dashoffset, one coral moving dot, no external imagery.
- **Sound intent:** Music reaches its most active middle passage here. The signal dot may coincide with a soft percussive accent, but beat alignment is unverified.

## Variety and pacing review

- The five major shots alternate photographic, typographic, diagrammatic, social-system, and brand-lockup modes.
- Adjacent shots differ in at least two dimensions: Shot 01 asymmetric photo/horizon, Shot 02 full-bleed photo/lens, Shot 03 dark network/central trace, Shot 04 cream constellation/low band, Shot 05 dark centered lockup/aperture.
- Every shot has a post-entrance event: rings expand, the contour straightens, the evidence path branches, the constellation breathes, and the final horizon contracts.
- Beat density is at least one visible change per second, with multiple discrete changes in the opener. No shot ends on a bare background; each has a carrier already moving into the next shot.
- Headline, supporting copy, hero/photo, and callouts occupy separate zones. Labels arrive sequentially rather than as a simultaneous stack.
- No single photo plate appears in more than two shots; the supplied photo is used in Shots 01–02 only. The remaining shots use distinct graphic fields.
- No claim is invented beyond the project's existing purpose and supplied language. The film uses mission framing rather than unsupported statistics or outcomes.
