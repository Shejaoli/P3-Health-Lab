Use only supported project descriptions and verified assets. Available material does not verify participation totals, media coverage, policy outcomes, impact metrics, or funder logos. P3 Health Lab and Western University may be shown as affiliations, not as funders.

**Why:** The page brief requests evidence and partner/funder material, but unverified claims or logos could misrepresent the project.

**How to apply:** Add metrics, outcomes, media/policy evidence, or funder branding only after an approved source and attribution are supplied.
